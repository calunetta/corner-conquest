No code was written or edited for this catalog.

## New capability unlocks

Cross-checked against `docs/gameplay-ideas.md` (all 15 ideas, batches 1-4):

- **Idea 3 — Roaming monster (shared threat):** This pack directly supports giving the roaming monster a distinct, more "world-boss"-feeling identity than the static island monsters. `Enemy Pack/Troll/` has a unique `Troll_Windup.png` and `Troll_Recovery.png` (a telegraphed heavy attack sequence, not present on any current monster), which fits a "roaming threat that telegraphs its approach" flavor well. `Enemy Pack/Extra/Minotaur Guard/Minotaur_Guard.png` and `Panda Guard/Panda_Guard.png` are also distinct "guard" variants of existing creatures that could be reused instead of fully new art.
- **Idea 10 — Base siege / capture-adjacent mechanic:** `Factions/Knights/Buildings/Castle/Castle_Construction.png` and `Castle_Destroyed.png`, and `Factions/Goblins/Buildings/Wood_Tower/Wood_Tower_Destroyed.png` / `Wood_House_Destroyed.png`, provide ready-made visual states for a besieged/damaged base that don't exist today (current `castle_${color}.png` has no damaged variant). Directly unlocks the visual half of idea 10 without new art.
- **Idea 11 — Weather/terrain variety per island:** `Terrain/Ground/Tilemap_Elevation.png` and `Tilemap_Flat.png`, plus `Terrain/Bridge/Bridge_All.png`, give actual terrain-tile variety (elevation, flat ground, bridges) the current game has none of — islands today are undifferentiated beyond resource type. This is the clearest "unlocks a currently-impossible visual" match in the whole pack.
- **Idea 13 — Post-match meta-progression (cosmetic skins/store):** This is the single biggest match for the pack's breadth. The 4-color, 5-unit-type `Units/` and `Factions/Knights/Troops/` sets, plus the entirely separate `Factions/Goblins/` faction (different building and troop silhouettes, e.g. `Goblin_House.png`, `Barrel_{Color}.png`, `TNT_{Color}.png`, `Torch_{Color}.png`), are exactly the kind of "alternate army/castle skins" idea 13's revised store concept calls for. Goblins vs. Knights could become two purchasable cosmetic factions rather than just recolors.
- **Idea 8 — Asymmetric faction traits:** The existence of two structurally different factions (Knights: Warrior/Pawn/Archer/Lancer/Monk; Goblins: Barrel/TNT/Torch troops with explosive/fire themes) could inspire the *flavor* of asymmetric traits (e.g. a "Goblin" player gets a fire/explosive-themed bonus) even though idea 8 as written doesn't require new art — this pack makes a more thematic version of it possible.
- **Idea 14 — Scored combat log / replay highlights:** No direct asset unlock; this idea is pure data/log processing and doesn't need new sprites.
- **Ideas 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 12, 15:** No specific asset dependency identified — these are rules/data/UI-logic changes (trade, raiding rewards, ceasefires, yield curves, event deck, catch-up bonus, intel trading, disconnect handling, bot difficulty) that don't hinge on new art. The pack doesn't block or unlock any of them.

## Unused/surplus categories

- `public/tiny-swords/Effects/` and `public/tiny-swords/Particle FX/` (fire, explosion, dust, water-splash sprite sheets) — available, unused; no current combat/ability VFX layer exists to consume them.
- `public/tiny-swords/UI/` and `public/tiny-swords/UI Elements/` (buttons, banners, ribbons, bars, cursors, human avatars, numbered icon sets) — available, unused; the game's UI is Tailwind/shadcn (CSS-based), not sprite-based, per `CLAUDE.md`'s stack section.
- `public/tiny-swords/Terrain/Tileset/`, `Terrain/Ground/`, `Terrain/Bridge/` (full tilemap/elevation/bridge tile sets) — available, unused beyond the idea-11 unlock noted above; the game's map today is a flat grid of island tiles with no tile-level terrain rendering.
- `public/tiny-swords/Deco/` (18 generic numbered decoration PNGs) — available, unused; no inspection done on individual contents.
- `public/tiny-swords/Factions/Goblins/Buildings/` and `Troops/` (Wood_House, Wood_Tower, Barrel, TNT, Torch per color) — available, unused outside the idea-13 cosmetic-store unlock noted above.
- `public/tiny-swords/Enemy Pack/{Bomb Fish, Bumblebee, Giant Bat, Gnoll, Gnome, Harpoon Shark, Hex Shaman, Imp, Lizard, Paddle Shark, Panda, Skull, Slingshot Gnome, Snake, Thief, Torch Goblin, Turtle}/` — available, unused; 17 more creature types beyond the 4 the game currently names, with no documented plan to add monster variety beyond the existing Lvl 1-4 ladder.
- `public/tiny-swords/Enemy Pack/Extra/{Cannon, Cave, Fish Hut, Gnome Buildings, Goblin Hut, Pig, Pig Rider Spear Goblin, Pirate Tower, Skull decorations, Wooden Fence}/` — available, unused; misc environmental/building props with no current game concept to attach to.
- `.aseprite` source files throughout the pack (e.g. `Bear.aseprite`, `Minotaur.aseprite`, `Tree.aseprite`) — these are Aseprite project files, not directly usable by `scripts/asset-tools/sprite-to-gif.mjs` (which presumably works from PNG sprite sheets); useful only if frame boundaries need to be re-verified visually in Aseprite rather than inferred from PNG dimensions.
